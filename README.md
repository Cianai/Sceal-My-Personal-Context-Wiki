# Sceal: a personal context wiki

**Working title:** Quantified (Self) Human AI Alignment: End-User informed and practical Evaluations through OpenWiki Personal Mode Inspired Context Engineering.

The question: does giving a language model a condensed, source-linked account of one person's own data make its reading of that person closer to what they meant than the same information given as raw text of equal length?

## What is here

| Path | What it is |
| -- | -- |
| [`study/`](study/) | Study materials: prompts, marking rubric, judge prompt, analysis script and its tests |
| [`evals/alteri-port/`](evals/alteri-port/) | The first prototype's comparison, ported to one small Inspect task, with its result |

## Status

Design and materials only. No results yet, and the study is not yet registered. The wiki itself will be generated with [OpenWiki](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/openwiki/overview) from the author's own sources and added here. Raw personal data and other people's content will never be.

The research question and design are the author's. The materials in `study/` and `evals/` were drafted with an AI research assistant to that design.
